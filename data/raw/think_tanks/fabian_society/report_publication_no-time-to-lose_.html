No time to lose: Six ways to tackle gender inequality

Share

  • No time to lose
  • By Georgie Nel , Iggy Wood , Miles Ward
  • Published 28 November 2025
  • Topics Women

The Fabian Women’s Network mentoring programme has been running for 14 years. Cohort 13 was the first group to witness Labour in government. In this collection, six members of Cohort 13 set out policy ideas in areas in need of more gender equality. The ideas were originally presented during a workshop in parliament.

The essays tackle contemporary topics ranging from the gender pensions gap through to online trolling. Featuring a foreword by Catherine Fookes MP, parliamentary private secretary to the prime minister, the collection ranges from the ‘gender pension gap’ to online trolling.
